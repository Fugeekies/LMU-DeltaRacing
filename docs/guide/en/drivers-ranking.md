# Classement des coureurs (*Head-to-head*)

Your **head-to-head** record against each driver met in a race.

- **Courses affrontées**, **Victoires** and **Défaites** (*races faced, wins, losses*): races against them, finished ahead of or behind them.
- **% de victoire** (*win rate*), with a green and red bar.
- **Cote**: their current [driver rating](driver-rating.md).
- **Écart moyen** (*average gap*): your average places ahead of them (green) or behind (red).
- **Contacts** between your two cars, and **Dernière course** (*last race*) against them.

Only **races** in **your class** count. Search a driver by name and sort by races, wins, win rate or name.

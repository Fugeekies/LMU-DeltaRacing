# Head-to-head

Your **head-to-head** record against each driver met in a race.

- **Races faced**, **Wins** and **Losses**: races against them, finished ahead of or behind them.
- **Win rate**, with a green and red bar.
- **Rating**: their current [driver rating](driver-rating.md).
- **Average gap**: your average places ahead of them (green) or behind (red).
- **Contacts** between your two cars, and **Last race** against them.

Only **races** in **your class** count. Search a driver by name and sort by races, wins, win rate or name.

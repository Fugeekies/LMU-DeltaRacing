# Home

Your career at a glance, shown at startup. Each tile gives a total, then the detail by category.

- **Driver**: your name, first and last races, most driven track and car, and your [driver rating](driver-rating.md).
- **Recent form**: your last 10 races as dots and a link to the last one.
- **Record**: races, wins, podiums, top 5, top 10 and average position.
- **Mileage**: laps, kilometres and time on track.
- **Qualifying & start**: poles, average qualifying position, places gained in the race.
- **Retirements**: retirements, disqualifications, unclassified races and their causes.

> Results count your **position in your class**: a LMGT3 win in a multi-class race is a win. A retirement counts in the total, not in the results.

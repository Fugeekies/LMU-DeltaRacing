# Progression (*Progress*)

A chart of how your best laps evolve on a track, in a class.

- Pick the **circuit** (*track*) and **catégorie** (*class*): the page opens on those of your last race.
- **Période** (*Period*): day, week, month or year.
- **Valeur tracée** (*Plotted value*): the best (min), the average or the worst (max) of each period; the coloured band goes from min to max.
- **Affichage** (*Display*): one Race curve and one Qualifying curve, or one curve per car.
- The **summary** gives, for each curve, your record, the starting value, the current value and the gap.
- Click a legend entry to hide a curve; **Afficher le détail des périodes** (*Show period details*) gives the exact values.

Your choices are remembered for next time.

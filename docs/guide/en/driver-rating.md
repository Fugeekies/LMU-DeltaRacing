# Driver rating

Your level against the drivers you meet, measured by an **Elo rating**, as in chess. Each race is a series of duels with the drivers of your class: beating a higher-rated driver earns a lot, finishing behind a lower-rated one costs a lot.

- **Your rating**: rank, tier (**Platinum** top 10%, **Gold** up to 30%, **Silver** up to 60%, **Bronze** beyond) and record.
- **Next goals**: the points needed to pass the next driver and reach the next tier.
- **Evolution**, best and worst races, **history** (expected place, field strength, points won) and the **ranking** of all drivers.

Everyone starts at 1,500; during your first 5 races, the rating moves twice as fast and you are not ranked yet. Only races count. The rating is computed from your own files only: it is not LMU's official ranking.

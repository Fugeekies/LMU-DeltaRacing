# Lap-by-lap analysis

The analysis of one race, lap by lap.

1. Choose the **race**: only yours are listed, the most recent first.
2. Choose the **driver**: you by default, or any other driver of the race to compare.

For that driver, the page gives their **pit stops**, **consistency** (standard deviation of their representative laps, with their rank in the class), **pace** and **top speed**, then the chart of their lap times (lower is faster, yours in red to compare). The **stints** table splits the race at each pass through the pit lane: tyres, pace and best lap of each stint. **Consumption and wear** gives the fuel and energy used per lap, the laps a full tank lasts and the tyre wear, in percent, when the server shares these data. **Show lap details** gives every lap.

Below, **Personal records** gives your best lap and top speed with each car on the circuit of the race, in the class you drove, and **Compare sessions** lists all your sessions there with their gap to the record, their pace, their consistency and a **Record** badge when a session beats your record with that car.

In **Race details**, the **Lap-by-lap analysis of … →** link below the race story opens this page on that race and driver.

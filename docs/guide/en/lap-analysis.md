# Lap-by-lap analysis

The analysis of one race, lap by lap.

1. Choose the **race**: only yours are listed, the most recent first.
2. Choose the **driver**: you by default, or any other driver of the race to compare.

The tiles at the top sum up their race: **Pace** (median of their representative laps), **Consistency** (standard deviation, with their rank in the class), **Ideal lap** (sum of their three best sectors), **Top speed**, **Pit stops** and, when the server shares it, **Fuel / lap**.

**Sector by sector** shows where the time goes: for each sector, the best time (bold when it is the best of the class), the pace compared with the class reference (the best median in that sector), the consistency and the **time lost per lap**, with a red bar. A sentence names the sector that costs the most.

**Lap by lap** plots the time of every lap, or of one sector with the **S1**, **S2** and **S3** buttons (lower is faster, yours in red to compare). The **Laps** table gives every lap with its three sectors (bold for the driver's best), its position, its top speed and, when the server shares them, fuel, energy and tyre wear. **Stints and consumption** splits the race at each pass through the pit lane: tyres, pace, consumption and wear of each stint.

Below, **Personal records** gives your best lap and top speed with each car on the circuit of the race, in the class you drove, and **Compare sessions** lists all your sessions there with their gap to the record, their pace, their consistency and a **Record** badge when a session beats your record with that car.

The last column of **Compare sessions**, **NOTES**, recalls the conditions, feeling and setup noted in [Race details](race-details.md); hover over it to read the whole note.

In **Race details**, the **🔬 Lap-by-lap analysis** button, to the right of the race story settings, opens this page on that race and driver.

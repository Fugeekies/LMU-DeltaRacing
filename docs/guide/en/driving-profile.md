# Profil de conduite (*Driving profile*)

How you drive, over all your qualifying sessions and races, compared with the other drivers of those sessions.

- **Ce que vos incidents vous coûtent** (*What your incidents cost you*): time lost on laps with a contact, against your median lap, and contacts on the first lap, with damage or followed by a retirement.
- **Votre bilan** (*Your record*): contacts, obstacles, track-limit warnings, invalidated laps and penalties per 100 laps. Green when you do at least as well as the others.
- **Où progresser** (*Where to improve*): the same figures by session type, class and track, with the risk zone of each track.
- **Forme récente** (*Recent form*), the drivers you hit most, obstacles hit and penalty reasons.

A collision counts once, even when both cars reported it. The game only reports invalidated laps in practice and qualifying.

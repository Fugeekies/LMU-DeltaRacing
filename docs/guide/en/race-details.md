# Détail des courses (*Race details*)

The full classification of an event.

1. Pick the event in the list: it groups the practice, qualifying and race run back to back on a track.
2. Switch sessions with the **COURSE** (*Race*), **QUALIFICATION** and **ESSAIS** (*Practice*) tabs.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **POS** / **CLASSE** | Overall position / class |
| **PILOTE** | Driver name and, below, car |
| **SECTEUR 1, 2, 3** | Best sector: 🟣 best in class, 🟡 standard time; below, the gap to the best |
| **TOUR IDÉAL** | Ideal lap: sum of the three best sectors, and its gap |
| **MEILLEUR TOUR** | Actual best lap, and its gap |
| **TEMPS TOTAL** | Race time and gap to the class leader: **LEADER**, a time, **+1 tour** (*lap*) or **DNF** |
| **CONTACTS** | Contacts with a car and, below, with an obstacle; click to see the incidents |
| **LIMITES** | Track-limit warnings and, below, invalidated laps |

Below the table, the **déroulé de la course** (*race story*) plots every car's position lap by lap: your line in white with your incidents and pit stops, a cross for a retirement. Click a line to follow that driver; **Autour de vous** (*Around you*) and the **−** / **+** buttons keep only the cars that finished near you.

Every comparison is made **within each class**, as in WEC. Your row is tagged **VOUS** (*you*).

# Race details

The full classification of an event.

1. Pick the event in the list: it groups the practice, qualifying and race run back to back on a track.
2. Switch sessions with the **RACE**, **QUALIFYING** and **PRACTICE** tabs.

Above the classification, the **📝 NOTES** strip keeps your notes on the session of the tab: **+ Add a note**, then the **CONDITIONS** (☀️ Dry, 🌥️ Damp, 🌧️ Rain, 🌦️ Mixed), your **FEELING** in stars, the **CAR**, the **SETUP** and **REMARKS**, all optional. **Save** keeps the note, **Edit** reopens it, **Delete the note** clears it. If you fitted wet tyres, the application offers **Choose Rain**. Notes stay on your PC, in the `race-notes.json` file next to the application.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **POS** / **CLASS** | Overall position / class |
| **DRIVER** | Driver name and, below, car |
| **SECTEUR 1, 2, 3** | Best sector, in bold when it is the best in class; below, the gap to the best |
| **IDEAL LAP** | Ideal lap: sum of the three best sectors, and its gap |
| **BEST LAP** | Actual best lap, and its gap |
| **TOTAL TIME** | Race time and gap to the class leader: **LEADER**, a time, **+1 lap** or **DNF** |
| **CONTACTS** | Contacts with a car and, below, with an obstacle; click to see the incidents |
| **LIMITS** | Track-limit warnings and, below, invalidated laps |

Below the table, the **race story** plots every car's position lap by lap: your line in white with your incidents and pit stops, a cross for a retirement. Click a line to follow that driver; **Around …** and the **−** / **+** buttons keep only the cars that finished near you.

The [**Lap-by-lap analysis of … →**](lap-analysis.md) link below the race story opens the analysis of the followed driver: consistency, stints, consumption and wear.

Every comparison is made **within each class**, as in WEC. Your row is tagged **YOU**.

# User guide

LMU DeltaRacing reads the result files that *Le Mans Ultimate* saves after each session and turns them into a personal dashboard. Everything stays on your PC: **no data is collected**.

> The application speaks English, French, German, Italian and Spanish: choose the language in **Settings › Language and date**.

## Dashboard pages

| Page | In short |
|---|---|
| [Home](home.md) | Your career at a glance |
| [My races](my-races.md) | Your history, track by track |
| [Race details](race-details.md) | The full classification of an event, sector by sector |
| [Progress](progression.md) | How your best laps evolve on a track |
| [Driving profile](driving-profile.md) | Your contacts, track limits and penalties |
| [Driver rating](driver-rating.md) | Your level, measured by an Elo rating |
| [Head-to-head](drivers-ranking.md) | Your record against each opponent |

**In every table**, drag the handle on the right edge of a header to widen or narrow a column; double-click that edge to restore the original widths.

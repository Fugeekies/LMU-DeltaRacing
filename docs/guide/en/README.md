# User guide

LMU DeltaRacing reads the result files that *Le Mans Ultimate* saves after each session and turns them into a personal dashboard. Everything stays on your PC: **no data is collected**.

> The application is in French. This guide quotes the on-screen labels as they appear, followed by their translation: **Mes courses** (*My races*).

## Getting started

- [Installation and first launch](installation.md)
- [Settings](settings.md)
- [FAQ](faq.md)

## Dashboard pages

| Page | In short |
|---|---|
| [Accueil](home.md) (*Home*) | Your career at a glance |
| [Mes courses](my-races.md) (*My races*) | Your history, track by track |
| [Détail des courses](race-details.md) (*Race details*) | The full classification of an event, sector by sector |
| [Progression](progression.md) (*Progress*) | How your best laps evolve on a track |
| [Profil de conduite](driving-profile.md) (*Driving profile*) | Your contacts, track limits and penalties |
| [Cote pilote](driver-rating.md) (*Driver rating*) | Your level, measured by an Elo rating |
| [Classement des coureurs](drivers-ranking.md) (*Head-to-head*) | Your record against each opponent |

**In every table**, drag the handle on the right edge of a header to widen or narrow a column; double-click that edge to restore the original widths.
